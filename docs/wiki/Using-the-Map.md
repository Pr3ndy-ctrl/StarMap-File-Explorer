# Using the map

## Moving around

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Turn the map | Drag |
| Zoom | Scroll, or pinch on a trackpad or touch screen |
| Move the map sideways | Shift-drag, or right-drag |
| See a file's or folder's details | Click its star or name |
| Fly to a star | Double-click it |
| Go back to the whole map | **Whole sky** |
| Close the right-hand panel | **×**, or press Esc |

The map turns slowly on its own until you touch it. Untick **Slow rotation** to stop that.

## What you're looking at

- **Stars are files.** Bigger files are bigger and brighter.
- **Clusters are folders.** Each folder's subfolders are smaller clusters inside it, and its name floats above it. More names appear as you zoom in.
- **Violet nebulae** are app bundles and folders full of machine-managed files, such as `node_modules`, `.git`, Library or AppData. They're drawn as one cloud each so they don't hide your own files. Click one to see how many files and how much space it holds.
- **Faint blue lines** connect each folder to the folder it sits in. Untick **Folder lines** to hide them.
- **Gold arcs** join folders that hold copies of the same files. Brighter arcs mean more copies. Untick **Copy arcs** to hide them.

## Colours

Choose what the colours mean under **Colour stars by**:

- **Type:** writing, code, images, audio, video, data, archives and other. Click a type in the list to hide or show those stars.
- **Age:** blue for files changed most recently, through cream and orange, to red for the oldest.
- **Region:** one colour per top-level folder, such as Documents or Downloads. Click a region in the list to fly to it.

## Finding things

Type in **Find a file or folder name**. Every match lights up and everything else dims; the list underneath shows the most recently changed matches. Click one to fly to it.

## The panels

- **The left panel** holds the totals, search, colours and switches. On a narrow window it folds away; tap **key** to open it.
- **The right panel** shows whatever you clicked: a file with its preview, a folder report, a recommendation, or a report such as [Where the space goes](Where-the-Space-Goes).
- **The bar across the top** shows how full the disk is, when the last scan was, the **Re-scan** button and the [recommendations](Recommendations).
- **The readouts** beside the left panel and at the top right show disk, memory and network. See [Live readouts](Live-Readouts).

## Folder reports

Click a folder's name or cluster to see its files and size, space by file type, the bundles inside it, files not changed in over a year, how much of it is copied elsewhere, its largest files and the folders it shares copies with.
